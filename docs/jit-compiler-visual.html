Compiler boundary

Compilation is not synthesis.

ECIR states the contract. The compiler checks and lowers it. Untrusted components may fill open search slots, but cannot rewrite the admission rule.

Honest claim boundary

What the kernel proves—and what it cannot.

Conditional control-plane guarantees are useful. They are not a shortcut to truth, verifier soundness, or general intelligence.

Kernel guarantees

Conditional on the trusted boundary and declared dependencies.

  • ✓No claim admission without a satisfied registered policy.
  • ✓No proposer self-attestation when authority separation is required.
  • ✓No authorized effect outside the contract’s exact permission set.
  • ✓Declared transitive dependents become unavailable after retraction.
  • ✓Historical ledger mutation is detectable with an authentic head.

Empirical burden

Claims that only hostile experiments and real deployments can answer.

  • ?Did elaboration capture the user’s actual intent?
  • ?Does the verifier measure what the claim means?
  • ?Are real dependencies complete under distribution shift?
  • ?Do inferred capability guards reject adversarial near-matches?
  • ?Does proposer intervention loss decline after all lifecycle costs?
